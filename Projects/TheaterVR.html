Timeline Theater VR

An Immersive Journey Through the History of Computing

Overview

Timeline Theater VR is an educational virtual reality application developed with Unity for Meta Quest. The experience transforms the history of computing into an immersive journey, allowing users to discover major technological milestones through an interactive timeline and detailed 3D visualizations. Rather than reading information from a textbook, users navigate a virtual theater where each period of computing history is presented through interactive exhibits, historical machines, and informative presentations.
